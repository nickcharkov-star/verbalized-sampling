## What went wrong, and why

1. **Drive was declared unreachable last session. It was not.**
   *Root cause:* an untested assumption — that a large download would come
   back into the conversation. The harness saves oversize tool results to a
   file, which is a clean path to disk.
   *Rule:* `.claude/skills/drive-files/SKILL.md` — try the transfer before
   declaring it impossible.

2. **The print's last movement first rebuilt "Celestial" as a brown blob in
   its silhouette.** Caught in the screenshots before shipping.
   *Root cause:* a silhouette filled with another photograph's colours is a
   mock-up of the product, which is the one thing this user has ruled out.
   *Rule:* `.claude/skills/pixel-field/SKILL.md` — end on a second
   photograph, not a silhouette.

3. **The phone overflow test passed while the page was 41 px too wide.**
   *Root cause:* it compared `scrollWidth` with `innerWidth`, and on a phone
   `innerWidth` grows with the overflowing content. The fanned cover cards
   were the culprit.
   *Rule:* measure against `documentElement.clientWidth` and
   `visualViewport.width` (now in `.claude/skills/domain-black/SKILL.md`).

4. **The theme had drifted into a different brand voice** ("Maison Domain ·
   Oslo", "lots", "issued to bearer", "settle").
   *Root cause:* storefront templates were never re-read when the concept
   changed. *Rule:* the site is the source; `scripts/shopify/sync-from-site.py`
   carries it into the theme, and a copy audit (grep for the old vocabulary)
   runs before shipping.

## What to keep

- Contact sheets for reviewing photographs: fifteen frames for the cost of one.
- The degrade matrix (no WebGL, each module blocked, CDN down, reduced
  motion, no JavaScript) — it is what proves the page reads.
- Heavy parts behind `import()`; content in static HTML.

## Flaws fixed in the design

The dark ground; three type families competing; a hero built on a 270-px
frame; low-resolution imagery; decorative rules that encoded nothing; the
duplicated issue line in the header; Bodoni's missing W kerning; a print
caption that read "The Fire" in italic; the drawn approximation of the
monogram in the seal (now a photograph of the real one); a stage photo
floating in a too-wide column; china-marker rings that cut through
neighbouring frames; a seal that spun the logo (now only the ring turns).
