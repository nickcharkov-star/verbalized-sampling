Composition

Built to open slowly.

Head

First twenty minutes

Bergamot peel and pink pepper, kept dry. It reads bright for about as long as it takes to leave the house, then gets out of the way.

Heart

Hours two to five

Orris butter and a warm, powdery jasmine. This is the part the house is actually selling; everything else is approach and exit.

Base

What stays on the coat

Sandalwood, benzoin, a little ambrette. Still legible on wool the next morning, which is the only longevity claim worth making.
