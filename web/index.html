Domain BlackThe Beliefp. 04

From the founder

We create fragrance to reveal what is already known.

Domain Black began with a simple belief: fragrance is worn to be remembered, not to be noticed.

Every composition in the House is built the same way: a mood first, a material second, a scent last. It is why the five do not smell like variations on one another. They are five rooms, not five shades.

We believe fragrance is more than scent. It is presence, memory, and identity made tangible. The confidence. The presence. The power to step into your Domain.

Kamaala Emmanuel — Founder
